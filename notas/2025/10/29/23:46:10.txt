*(Circa 1776, as it might have been spoken by an Indigenous orator)*

Therefore, by the voice of our ancestors and the beating of our hearts, we declare that we are, and have always been, **a free and sovereign people**. No power born of paper or crown may own the spirit of the earth or the will of its first guardians.
