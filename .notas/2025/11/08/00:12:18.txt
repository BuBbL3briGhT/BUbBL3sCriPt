🎶 Ci sono case e chiese che Dio benedica

(Italian–English Lyric Sheet with Pronunciation)


---

Ci sono case e chiese che Dio benedica,
There are houses and churches that God blesses,
(chee SOH-no KAH-zeh eh KYEH-zeh keh DEE-oh beh-neh-DEE-kah)

Lassù, Lui le ha costruite, le ha costruite,
Up above, He built them, He built them,
(lah-SOO, LOO-ee leh ah koh-stroo-EE-teh, leh ah koh-stroo-EE-teh)

Vi dico che l'ha fatto, tutto con amore,
I tell you He made it all with love,
(vee DEE-koh keh lah FAHT-toh, TOOT-toh kohn ah-MOH-reh)

Entrateci e troverete pace, non c'è dolore.
Enter in and you will find peace, there is no pain.
(en-TRAH-teh-chee eh troh-veh-REH-teh PAH-cheh, non cheh doh-LOH-reh)


---

Lavorare insieme, e amatevi gli uni gli altri,
Work together, and love one another,
(lah-voh-RAH-reh een-SYEH-meh, eh ah-MAH-teh-vee lyee OO-nee lyee AHL-tree)

Con il cuore pieno di fede, senza più timore,
With hearts full of faith, without any more fear,
(kohn eel KWOH-reh PYEH-no dee FEH-deh, SEHN-zah pyoo tee-MOH-reh)

Siamo tutti fratelli, sotto lo stesso cielo,
We are all brothers under the same sky,
(SYAH-moh TOOT-tee frah-TEL-lee, SOHT-toh loh STEHS-soh CHYEH-loh)

Fratelli e sorelle, nel nostro cammino, al fianco di Dio.
Brothers and sisters, in our journey, beside God.
(frah-TEL-lee eh soh-REL-leh, nehl NOHS-troh kahm-MEE-noh, al FYAHN-koh dee DEE-oh)


---

Perché ognuno di noi è solo fratello,
Because each of us is only a brother,
(pehr-KEH ohn-YOO-noh dee noy eh SOH-loh frah-TEL-loh)

Nessuno è solo, siamo una famiglia,
No one is alone, we are a family,
(neh-SOO-noh eh SOH-loh, SYAH-moh oo-nah fah-MEEL-yah)

Creatura di Dio, insieme camminiamo,
Creatures of God, we walk together,
(kreh-ah-TOO-rah dee DEE-oh, een-SYEH-meh kahm-mee-NYAH-moh)

Uniti nell’amore, non ci abbandoniamo.
United in love, we do not abandon each other.
(oo-NEE-tee nehl-lah-MOH-reh, non chee ahb-bahn-doh-NYAH-moh)


---

Fratelli e sorelle, l’intera razza umana,
Brothers and sisters, the entire human race,
(frah-TEL-lee eh soh-REL-leh, leen-TEH-rah RAHT-tsah oo-MAH-nah)

In questo mondo di speranza, di fede che non svanisce,
In this world of hope, of faith that never fades,
(een KWEHS-toh MOHN-doh dee speh-RAHN-tsah, dee FEH-deh keh non svah-NEE-sheh)

Siamo tutti figli di Dio, camminiamo nella Sua luce,
We are all children of God, we walk in His light,
(SYAH-moh TOOT-tee FEEL-yee dee DEE-oh, kahm-mee-NYAH-moh NEHL-lah SOO-ah LOO-cheh)

Con amore, con forza, con cuore che non tradisce.
With love, with strength, with hearts that do not betray.
(kohn ah-MOH-reh, kohn FOHR-tsah, kohn KWOH-reh keh non trah-DEE-sheh)


---

Ci sono case e chiese che Dio benedica,
There are houses and churches that God blesses,
(chee SOH-no KAH-zeh eh KYEH-zeh keh DEE-oh beh-neh-DEE-kah)

E nel Suo abbraccio troviamo forza e fede,
And in His embrace we find strength and faith,
(eh nehl SOO-oh ahb-BRAH-choh troh-VYAH-moh FOHR-tsah eh FEH-deh)

Entriamo insieme, l’unione è la nostra forza,
We enter together, unity is our strength,
(en-TREE-ah-moh een-SYEH-meh, loo-NYOH-neh eh lah NOHS-trah FOHR-tsah)

E tutto sarà fatto, nel Suo nome, amen.
And all will be done, in His name, amen.
(eh TOOT-toh sah-RAH FAHT-toh, nehl SOO-oh NOH-meh, AH-mehn)


---
